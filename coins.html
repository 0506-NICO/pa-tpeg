Community Coin Program

Think Plan Execute Coins

A coin program from Think Plan Execute Global Inc that recognizes service, marks milestones, and raises support for the communities we serve.

Ask about coins See the collections
The idea

A coin that means something

Each Think Plan Execute Coin stands for a commitment kept: a volunteer's service, a graduate's finished program, a leader's recognition, or a community's milestone. Coins are a way to honor people and to invite others into the mission.

Proceeds from the program support TPEG's charitable and educational programs.

Future expansion

More to come

We plan to expand the program over time. Any new product line, including products in other materials, will be offered only after the required legal, tax, and regulatory review.

Good to know

About purchases and receipts

Coins are physical commemorative items. They are not a currency or an investment. When you receive a coin in return for a payment, only the amount above the coin's fair market value may be tax-deductible, and your receipt will say so. A donation made without receiving a coin is fully deductible to the extent allowed by law.
