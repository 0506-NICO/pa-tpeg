Programs

Eight focus areas, one method

Different problems, same discipline: Think, Plan, Execute. Every program below runs through all three stages before it's called done.

Health & wellbeing

Support that meets people where they are

Autism

Autism management & education

Caregiver training, classroom support resources, and awareness programs for families and educators supporting autistic children and adults.

Behavioral health

Addiction, behavioral support & counseling

Referral pathways and peer support groups connecting people to licensed counselors and treatment providers. We coordinate care — we don't replace licensed clinicians.

Veterans

Veteran care

Transition support, family assistance, and referrals to health and benefits services for veterans and their households.

Community & justice

Showing up where it's easy to be forgotten

Emergency response

Food security & emergency preparedness

Food bank partnerships and household emergency-readiness training ahead of floods, fires, and other local disruptions.

Outreach

Food delivery & prison visitation

Meal delivery for shut-ins and families in crisis, plus visitation and reentry support for incarcerated individuals and the families they leave behind — run only through authorized correctional-service channels.

Advisory

Strategic planning & consulting

We put our own Think-Plan-Execute method to work for partner nonprofits, schools, and institutions that need help turning a mission into a workable plan.

Economic empowerment

Building income, not just relief

Enterprise

Entrepreneurship & job creation

Small-business incubation, micro-grant referrals, and mentorship for first-time founders, paired with local employer partnerships for direct hiring.

Skills

Vocational training

Hands-on trade and digital-skills training tracks designed to lead directly into paid work or a founded business.

Partner with us

Run one of these programs at your institution

Schools, universities, religious groups, hospitals, and clinics can bring any of these programs to their own community.
