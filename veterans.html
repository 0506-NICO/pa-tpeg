Veterans & family support

Support for those who served — and the families who served with them.

Think Plan Execute Global Inc brings its community programs to veterans and military families: wellness and resilience education, practical help, career and business training, and a clear path to the services they have earned.

Support veterans & families Ask for help or volunteer
What we offer

Practical help, built around the whole household

Wellness & resilience

Workshops for veterans and families

Education on stress management, emotional regulation, and family resilience, with peer support. These are educational programs; clinical care is provided only by appropriately licensed professionals, and we refer where needed.

Basic needs

Food, clothing & emergency help

Food and clothing support, disaster relief, and homeless outreach for veterans and their households facing hardship.

Careers & business

Job training & entrepreneurship

Vocational training, job-readiness coaching, mentorship, and entrepreneurship support to help service members move into civilian careers.

Navigation

Referrals to benefits & health services

Help finding and applying for health, housing, and benefits services, and connecting families with the right agency or provider.

Families

Family support

Parent and caregiver education, family coaching, and community connection for spouses, children, and caregivers.

Learning

Academy & exam support

Online courses, coaching, and exam preparation through the TPEG Academy.
