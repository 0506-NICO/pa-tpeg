TPEG Online Academy

Education without borders. Opportunity without limits.

The Online Education & Coaching Academy is the learning arm of Think Plan Execute Global Inc. It brings education, coaching, and skills development to youth, families, professionals, and underserved communities well beyond Maryland.

Academy tracks

Each track applies the same Think, Plan, Execute, Global method.

New — featured

Sports, fitness & coaching

Youth and adult programs in fitness coaching, sports leadership, athletic development, teamwork, sportsmanship, goal setting, and personal fitness planning. Coaching tracks include Youth Coach, Sports Mentor, Fitness Coach, and Wellness Coach.

New — featured

Physiotherapy & rehabilitation education

Educational courses on anatomy and physiology, posture, mobility, stretching, movement, ergonomics, injury prevention, and exercise fundamentals. This is education only. Hands-on clinical care is provided only by appropriately licensed professionals.

Certificates

What a certificate means here

Academy certificates (Participation, Completion, and Advanced) recognize completed learning. They are not government-issued professional licenses, degrees, or board certifications, and they do not qualify anyone to practice a regulated profession.
