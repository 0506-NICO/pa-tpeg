Our founder

Professor Dr. Patricia Chinwe O.O. Ejiogu

PhDs, DN, DO (Manual Osteopath, EU Style), LEHP — President and Founder, Think Plan Execute Global Inc.

Core mission

Dedicated to serving humanity

Dr. Ejiogu is dedicated to serving humanity, empowering underserved populations, advancing global health, and creating sustainable community transformation. Her personal guiding framework: Think. Plan. Execute. Serve. Transform.

She founded TPEG — Think Plan Execute Global Inc — to put that framework to work at scale, building an organization that turns intention into delivered outcomes for the communities it serves.

Affiliations

Global network

Affiliated with the International Circle of Faith (ICOF), alongside other global organizations, connecting TPEG's work to a wider international community of practice.

Credentials & education

A multidisciplinary foundation

Public health, psychology, divinity, and manual medicine — trained across the fields TPEG's programs draw on.

Public health & medical sciences

Professor of Public Health

Specializing in Medical Epidemiology and Clinical Toxicology. Licensed Medical Laboratory Scientist in Nigeria.

Psychology & behavioral health

Licensed Clinical Psychologist

Licensed in Nigeria, with an Associate Psychology license in Washington, D.C. Master's degree in Addiction Counseling. Doctoral degree in Psychology from Charisma University.

Divinity & counseling

Doctor of Divinity

Master's and Doctoral degrees in Divinity from Charisma University. Active Christian counselor.

Manual healthcare & rehabilitation

Doctor of Manual Osteopathy

Doctoral-level education and training in Naprapathy, Manual Osteopathy, and Rehabilitation Medicine via the National University of Medical Sciences (NUMSS).

Focus areas

Where she's put this training to work

Public health & healthcare

Disease prevention & holistic care

Disease prevention, early intervention, public health education, holistic care, and environmental/clinical risk mitigation.

Mental health & special needs

Addiction counseling & autism advocacy

Mental health awareness, addiction counseling, and specialized education and advocacy for autism and developmental needs.

Education & vocational training

Job creation & skills development

Programs for low-income and disadvantaged populations, focused on job creation, skills development, and entrepreneurship.

Humanitarian & social relief

Orphans, veterans & the incarcerated

Assistance to orphans, widows, veterans, and incarcerated individuals through prison visitation, rehabilitation, food and clothing donations, and shelter support.

Disaster response & outreach

Emergency relief & capacity building

Disaster relief, emergency medical supply distribution, housing assistance, and international capacity building.

Global entrepreneurship

Cross-border economic empowerment

Operates business ventures in Nigeria and the United States to drive economic empowerment and service delivery across borders.
