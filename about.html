About us

Why we exist, and how we work

We started from a frustration: too many good ideas in our communities never make it past a group chat. TPEG — Think Plan Execute Global — exists to close that gap. Every word in our name is deliberate: we Think before we act, we Plan before we spend, we Execute until it's delivered, and we do it at Global scale — our charter commits us to feeding the hungry and clothing the naked worldwide, not only within Maryland. TPEG is affiliated with Paham, our parent organization.

Mission

From intention to outcome, with nothing lost in between

TPEG Inc is a Maryland-incorporated 501(c)(3) nonprofit organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes, serving underprivileged men, women, veterans, and the homeless — through structured thinking, honest planning, and hands-on execution support.

We don't run one-off events. Every program we take on is tracked through all three of our stages, with a named owner and a public record of what was promised and what was delivered.

Vision

A generation that finishes what it starts

We want to be known less for the ideas we generate and more for the plans we actually see through — building a track record that communities and partners can trust with the next one.

How we work

The same four stages, every time

STAGE 01

Think

Listening sessions with the people affected, a written problem statement, and an honest look at what's already been tried.

STAGE 02

Plan

A plan with named owners, a timeline, and a public budget — reviewed before a dollar is spent or a volunteer is assigned.

STAGE 03

Execute

Delivery by field teams and volunteers, with a short report afterward on what worked, what didn't, and what changes next time.

STAGE 04

Global

Our charter commits us to this work worldwide, not only in Maryland. Think. Plan. Execute. Global — that's the whole name, and it's deliberate.
