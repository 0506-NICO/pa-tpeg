Institutional partnerships

For schools, universities, religious groups, hospitals & clinics

We work directly with institutions to run a program locally — vocational training inside a school, a food security drive through a place of worship, a veteran care referral desk in a clinic. Each partnership gets its own written plan with a named owner on both sides, following our usual Think–Plan–Execute process.
